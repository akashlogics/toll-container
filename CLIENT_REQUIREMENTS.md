# Kumar & Co. client requirements

Kumar & Co. needs a practical construction operations website for a contractor managing multiple projects and site teams. The contractor creates projects, adds employees with logins, assigns them to projects, and receives a reliable record of what happened on each site.

The primary daily workflows are site measurements and labour attendance. Measurements must support the paper register structure: BOQ item, room or work description, No, L, B, H, calculated quantity, rate, and amount. Deductions such as doors and ventilators may be entered as negative quantities. Labour must support the weekly register structure: date, named Mastri and Helper workers, overtime, food allowance, weekly totals, advances, deductions, TDS, and final payment.

The application therefore has three roles. The contractor/admin controls projects, employee accounts, worker roster, BOQ/rates, and reports. The manager reviews and approves measurements and labour before they count. The site supervisor enters daily measurements and named-worker attendance from the field. Alerts are available for assignments, approvals, and reminders.

The client’s examples refer to KTTRL Section 1, toll-road work areas, and operational measurement-sheet/reporting workflows. The software should preserve those concepts while allowing additional projects and work areas later.
